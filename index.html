About

I am a first-year Ph.D. student in the SETH Lab at Texas A&M, advised by Prof. Jeyavijayan (JV) Rajendran. I am a SCALE Graduate Fellow (U.S. Navy / Department of War) and a member of the Center for Secure Microelectronics Ecosystem (CSME).

I work on pre-silicon security verification for RISC-V processors: finding bugs in processor RTL before it becomes silicon. My current work combines LLM-guided analysis with formal model checking, and I have reported 264 previously unknown bugs in 23 open-source RISC-V cores, including CVA6, XiangShan, OpenC910, VeeR-EL2, and Tenstorrent's Ocelot.
