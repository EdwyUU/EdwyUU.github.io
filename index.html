About

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Engineering at Texas A&M University, working in the SETH Lab under Prof. Jeyavijayan (JV) Rajendran. I am affiliated with the Center for Secure Microelectronic Ecosystem (CSME).

My long-term goal is to become a professor, leading research in hardware security and computer architecture.

Research

My research focuses on LLM-driven hardware security verification for RISC-V processors. I am building an agentic framework that pairs LLM-generated security hypotheses with formal model checking to expose vulnerabilities before silicon is fabricated.
