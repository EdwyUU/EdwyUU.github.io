Research outputs

As of November 2026 I have reported 264 previously unknown bugs in 23 open-source RISC-V processors, and maintainers have confirmed 31 of them. Each one is filed as a public GitHub or GitLab issue with a test case that reproduces it.

Because this work is part of ongoing research, I am not publishing a detailed breakdown of these reports, such as which processors they affect or what kinds of bugs they are. I am glad to share the full statistics and the list of reports privately. Please email me at edwardazakov07@tamu.edu.
